Thank you for helping clean up Salem! Please read this page. When you sign the sign-in sheet (page 2), you are agreeing to it. A parent or guardian signs for anyone under 18 (page 3).

1. Know the risks

Picking up litter outdoors can involve cuts from glass or metal, needles, slips and falls, traffic, bugs, poison oak, bad weather, dirty or hazardous trash, and getting tired or sore. I choose to take part and accept these risks.

2. Stay safe

  • Listen to the safety talk and follow the event leader.
  • Wear closed-toe shoes. Use the grabbers, bags, and gloves provided.
  • Never touch needles, syringes, or anything hazardous. Mark the spot and tell a leader.
  • Stay off roads, out of the water, and with your group.
  • Stop and tell a leader right away if you get hurt or feel unwell.

We are not responsible. I take part at my own risk. To the fullest extent the law allows, I agree that Clean the Capital and its board, leaders, and volunteers are not responsible for any injury, illness, or lost or damaged property that happens while I volunteer, and I will not hold them liable, except for harm caused by their gross negligence or intentional misconduct.

3. First aid

If I am hurt and can't speak for myself, leaders may call 911 and give basic first aid. I am responsible for my own medical costs.

4. Photos

Clean the Capital may take photos at cleanups and use them on its website, social media, and grant reports, without payment. Don't want to be in photos? Check the "No photos" box next to your name.

5. Minors

Volunteers under 18 must have a parent or guardian sign for them on page 3, and an adult must stay with them for the whole event.

6. Volunteer, not employee

I am an unpaid volunteer. Clean the Capital may keep my sign-in info for its records and grant reports. We never share or publish volunteer names or contact info.

Clean the Capital is a volunteer group in Salem, Oregon. Questions: jacob.powell@cleanthecapital.org. Leaders: keep every signed sheet with this event's records (they are proof of volunteer hours for grants and the IRS). Do not post or photograph filled-in sheets.
