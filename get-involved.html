FAQ

Common questions

Do I need to bring supplies?

No. We provide gloves, grabbers, high-vis vests, and trash bags. Bring water, closed-toe shoes, and sun protection.

Can I bring my kids?

Yes — kids are welcome with a guardian. We'll route family groups on safer, lower-traffic paths.

Can I bring my dog?

Leashed dogs are welcome at most parks. Please check the specific location's rules and bring waste bags.

What if it rains?

We go anyway — it's Oregon. Only severe weather (lightning, ice) cancels. We'll email signed-up volunteers if a cleanup is called off.

I can only stay an hour. Is that OK?

Absolutely. Show up when you can, leave when you need to. Every bag matters.

Can my company or group host a cleanup?

Yes — reach out via the contact page. Group sponsorships and team-building cleanups are welcome.
