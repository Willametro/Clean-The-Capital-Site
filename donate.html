Before you give

An honest note about our status

Clean The Capital is not yet incorporated as a nonprofit. We are actively working through that process. Until 501(c)(3) status is granted, donations are not tax-deductible and are treated as personal gifts toward operating costs.

Every cent received is spent on supplies, public-bin contributions, and direct cleanup costs — never on personal compensation. We will publish a public ledger of donations and expenses on the transparency page.

If you need tax-deductible giving, consider an in-kind supply donation through a partner business, or check back once our nonprofit filings are complete.
