Our story

A cleaner Salem, built together.

Clean The Capital started with a simple observation: Salem is a beautiful city, and a lot of us walk past the same litter every day waiting for someone else to handle it. So we're handling it — together.

Mission

What we're doing

We organize free, bi-weekly Saturday cleanups in parks and public spaces around Salem. Anyone can show up. We hand out supplies, split into small walking groups, and spend a couple of hours making a visible difference.

But cleanups alone don't fix the underlying problem. So we're also pursuing two longer-term goals:

  • Funding and installing additional public trash cans in high-need spots
  • Partnering with local businesses, civic groups, and the City of Salem on ongoing cleaning services
Values

How we operate

  • Open to everyone. No fees, no political tests, no required commitment.
  • Visible and accountable. Every dollar and every hour is reported publicly.
  • Walkable and local. Meet points are public spaces with parking and transit nearby.
  • Safe and prepared. Safety briefings, vests, gloves, and grabbers provided.
  • Friendly to families. Kids and pets welcome with a guardian on safe routes.
Where we're headed

The longer plan

Year one — show up

Establish a reliable rhythm of cleanups, build a volunteer base, and prove the model works. That's now.

Year two — fund infrastructure

Pursue formal nonprofit status, secure partner sponsors, and begin funding public trash cans in identified hot spots.

Year three — sustain

Coordinate with the City of Salem on bin maintenance and recurring cleaning services so that the work outlasts any single volunteer.

Status: just getting started

We are not yet incorporated. Formal nonprofit setup is in progress. In the meantime, all support — time, supplies, partner relationships — goes directly into the cleanups themselves. Read our transparency note →
