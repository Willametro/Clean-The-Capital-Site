Our pillars

Three ways we're working on it

Cleanups are only the beginning. Our long-term project is building infrastructure and partnerships that keep Salem clean year-round.

Bi-weekly cleanups

Every other Saturday, 2–5 PM. We meet, hand out supplies, and walk together in small groups. Open to everyone — no signup fee, no experience needed.

More public trash cans

Litter happens where receptacles don't exist. We're mapping high-need spots and raising funds to install and maintain additional public bins across the city.

Partner-powered impact

Local businesses, civic groups, and city departments make this real. Sponsor supplies, host a meet point, or join our list of supporting partners.
