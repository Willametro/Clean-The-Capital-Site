All dates

Dates rotate through parks across Salem — flagship spots like Riverfront and the Capitol Mall, plus quieter neighborhood parks with more room and less foot traffic. New dates are added as the rotation continues into winter. Want the schedule emailed to you? Sign up here.

What to expect

How a cleanup runs

  • Arrive at the meet point a few minutes before 2 PM
  • Quick safety briefing and supply handout (5–10 minutes)
  • Split into small groups, each with a designated route
  • Walk and clean for about 2 hours
  • Regroup at the meet point to weigh in bags and wrap up
Come prepared

Bring with you

  • Water bottle (refillable if possible)
  • Closed-toe shoes and clothes you don't mind getting dirty
  • Sunscreen and a hat in warm months
  • A friend, neighbor, or family member — kids welcome with a guardian
