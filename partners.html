Ways to partner

How organizations help

Sponsor a cleanup

Cover the supplies, snacks, and logistics for a single Saturday cleanup. Your logo on event pages and signage at the meet point.

Donate supplies

Gloves, grabbers, vests, bags, water, sunscreen. In-kind donations are tracked publicly and acknowledged on this page.

Host a meet point

If your business has a parking lot or storefront on a cleanup route, host the meet point. We bring the people; you bring the address.

Send a team

Company outings, civic groups, faith communities, school clubs — bring your group and we'll route you together.

Fund a trash can

Help underwrite the cost of installing and maintaining a public trash can in a documented high-need location.

Civic collaboration

City departments, neighborhood associations, parks staff — let's coordinate so cleanups complement existing services.
