Transparency

Where we stand, where the money goes, and what we promise to report.

Current legal status

Clean The Capital is currently an unincorporated community initiative. Formal nonprofit incorporation (Oregon nonprofit corporation + federal 501(c)(3) recognition) is in progress. Until that's complete, donations are not tax-deductible.

How donations are handled

  • All donated funds are tracked separately from personal accounts.
  • Funds are only spent on direct project costs: supplies, public-bin contributions, event logistics, and infrastructure (e.g., this website, domain, email).
  • No one receives personal compensation from donated funds during the unincorporated phase.

Public reporting

Once we have meaningful activity to report, this page will host:

  • A running ledger of donations received (by amount and date; donor names only with consent).
  • An itemized list of expenses with receipts where applicable.
  • A bag-count and volunteer-hours summary after each cleanup.

First report will publish after our inaugural cleanup on May 30, 2026.
