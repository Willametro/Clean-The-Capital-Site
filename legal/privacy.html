Privacy

Plain language. Last updated October 2026.

What we collect

When you volunteer, donate, or send a message, we collect the information you give us — typically a name, email, and optional notes.

Website visit logs

When you load a page, a small script on this site sends us a visit log so we can see how people find us and spot spam bots. A log includes the page you visited, the site that referred you, how long you stayed, your IP address and approximate location (city and region), your network provider, and basic device details such as browser language, screen size, and time zone. The script also saves a random visitor ID in your browser's local storage so repeat visits can be grouped together. It does not record what you type into forms.

These logs are processed by Cloudflare and delivered to a private Discord channel that only our organizers can see. We use them only to understand site traffic and block abuse. We don't use them for advertising, and we don't sell or share them.

How we use it

We use your contact info to send reminders about cleanups you signed up for, respond to inquiries, and acknowledge donations. That's it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't share volunteer or donor lists with third parties.
  • We don't use advertising cookies, ad trackers, or third-party analytics services.

Third-party services

Form submissions and visit logs are processed by Cloudflare Workers. Form messages are delivered by email through Resend, visit logs are delivered to Discord, and maps are loaded via OpenStreetMap tiles. Those providers' privacy policies apply to data routed through them.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information, including visit logs, at any time. You can also clear this site's local storage in your browser to reset your visitor ID. Email jacob.powell@cleanthecapital.org.
