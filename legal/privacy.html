Privacy

Plain language. Last updated May 2026.

What we collect

When you volunteer, donate, or send a message, we collect the information you give us — typically a name, email, and optional notes. We do not use trackers, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics on this site.

How we use it

We use your contact info to send reminders about cleanups you signed up for, respond to inquiries, and acknowledge donations. That's it.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell or rent your information.
  • We don't share volunteer or donor lists with third parties.
  • We don't run advertising trackers on this site.

Third-party services

Form submissions are handled by Formspree, and maps are loaded via OpenStreetMap tiles. Both providers' privacy policies apply to data routed through them.

Your choices

You can ask us to delete your information at any time. Email jacob.powell@willametro.com.
